## Notes

- **B8 caveat.** The owner's 21–23 ms on 2 cores was not reproducible here (taskset to 2 cores makes the whole browser
  slower: 56 ms before). Evaluations per drag frame fell 146 000 → 76 000 (deterministic test); the timing margin on a
  slow 2-core device is unverified. The change trades drag-frame fidelity for dense fields only (decisions-log).
  The sparse reference field's drag bench fluctuates 14.5–21.8 ms on this noisy machine (it was 16.1 before); it is the
  same path with fewer evaluations.
- **B9** also drops the fixed `LINE_BEHIND_MIX`; the Phase 5 line/plane test now expects the physical value.
- **A2 (superseded 2026-10-31, see the per-value round):** the tolerance described here, treating eigenvalues closer than
  about 1e-6 of the matrix norm as equal, was removed. It merged widely spread spectra by mistake (a matrix with
  eigenvalues 1, 1.5 and 1000 returned error 0.25) and moved distinct eigenvalues by up to 5e-7 relative.
- Not asked, observed: `implicit-1600x1000-full` fluctuates around its 150 ms limit (127–155 ms).
